Add a "new dub to watch" badge when a dubbed episode drops ahead of you

- Cards now flip to a bright green "🆕 New dub · Ep N to watch" badge when the latest English
  dub episode is ahead of your progress on a show you're following (Watching / Caught up) — and
  clear the moment you bump your episode to it. Shows "🆕 K new dubs · Ep A–B" when you're several
  behind. Added to Weekly (compact chip + green card glow), List (meta badge) and Posters (corner chip).
- This pairs with the existing auto-caught-up: a card that read "Caught up" now both flips its
  status pill back to "Watching" AND shows the new-dub badge, then both revert once you catch up.
- "Behind" is now dub-based for dubbed anime: the old amber "▸ X behind" (which counted SUB episodes
  you don't watch) is replaced by the new-dub badge. Sub-only anime keep the sub-based "behind" count.
- Gated to anime, non-movie, Watching/Caught up only. New helpers newDubInfo()/newDubBadgeHTML();
  CSS .tag.newdub (pulse glow, reduced-motion aware), .wk-new, .pnew, .wcard.has-newdub.
- App version 2026.10.28a; service-worker cache bumped to v16.
