New "new dub to watch" badge, and un-mark Finished/Dropped by changing the episode

New-dub badge:
- Cards now flip to a bright green "🆕 New dub · Ep N to watch" badge when the latest English dub
  episode is ahead of your progress on a show you're following (Watching / Caught up) — and clear the
  moment you bump your episode to it. Shows "🆕 K new dubs · Ep A–B" when you're several behind. Added
  to Weekly (compact chip + green card glow), List (meta badge) and Posters (corner chip). Pairs with
  the existing auto-caught-up, so a "Caught up" card both flips to "Watching" AND shows the badge.
- "Behind" is now dub-based for dubbed anime: the old amber "▸ X behind" (which counted SUB episodes
  you don't watch) is replaced by the new-dub badge. Sub-only anime keep the sub-based "behind" count.

Un-mark a mistaken Finished/Dropped:
- Changing your episode count (＋/− stepper) on a show marked Finished or Dropped now moves it back to
  Watching (clearing fin_seasons/fin_eps), then auto-settles to Caught up if you're up to the dub.
  Any change triggers it; a toast confirms. Fixes a mis-tapped status without opening Edit. The Edit
  panel's explicit status dropdown is left untouched.
